### Promotional text

See nearby Bluetooth device names, compare signal strength, and follow warmer-or-colder changes. For accessories actively broadcasting BLE. Free and private.

### Description

Find your way toward a nearby Bluetooth Low Energy accessory by comparing its signal strength. Signal Scout helps you search for an item you own or have permission to locate, while it is powered on and actively advertising to your iPhone.

SEE WHAT IS NEARBY
Scan for BLE signals and see advertised device names, manufacturers, and measured signal strength (RSSI). Names and manufacturer information are clues, not verified identities. Some devices appear without these details.

COMPARE AS YOU MOVE
Choose a signal, take a few slow steps, and wait for fresh readings. Follow smoothed strength and warmer-or-colder trends. Clear paused, stale, and lost states help you distinguish current readings from historical data. Reset your comparison when you change position.

EXPLORE THE SIGNAL MAP
Stronger signals appear nearer the center. Expand, pan, zoom, or choose a device from the signal list. Dot angles provide visual spacing; they do not show compass direction.

PRIVATE AND SIMPLE
All features are available without an account, subscription, or time limit. Scanning and analysis happen on your iPhone. There is no location tracking, advertising, analytics, or signal upload. Optional haptic feedback can be switched off.

KNOW WHAT BLUETOOTH CAN TELL YOU
Signal strength is a relative clue, not exact distance. Walls, reflections, device orientation, and transmit power affect readings. A stronger signal alone does not identify your accessory.

Earbuds, headphones, speakers, and other accessories appear only when they advertise a compatible BLE signal. They may stop advertising while asleep, connected, or in a closed case. Silent and powered-off devices cannot appear.

Signal Scout does not provide GPS tracking, a finding network, or verified device ownership. It does not replace your accessory maker's finding service.

Requires an iPhone with iOS 17.0 or later and Bluetooth permission.
