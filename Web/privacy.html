PRIVACY POLICY

Private by design.

Privacy policy for version 1.3 (5) · Updated October 6, 2026

Summary

Signal Scout does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data. Bluetooth scanning and signal analysis happen on your device. The app has no developer-operated server, account system, analytics SDK, advertising SDK, or location code.

Bluetooth information

With your permission, Signal Scout receives Bluetooth Low Energy advertisements that iOS makes available to apps. It uses an app-scoped identifier, signal strength (RSSI), timestamps, and advertised service count to display and analyze nearby signals.

The app does not display or retain advertised device names or manufacturer data. Signal records remain in memory and are not written to a database or sent off the device. They disappear when the app process ends and can be removed from the current session with the Clear Inactive Devices control.

Free access and earlier versions

Version 1.3 (5) has no preview timer, subscription purchase, or StoreKit entitlement check. All features are available without payment. It does not read or write a preview start time.

Earlier versions stored a preview start time only in local app preferences and used Apple's StoreKit for subscriptions. A timestamp left by an earlier installation is ignored by this version and is removed when the app is deleted. Apple maintains any existing App Store purchase records under Apple's privacy policy; Signal Scout does not receive or store payment-card details.

Location, tracking, and sharing

Signal Scout does not request location permission, derive geographic coordinates, track you across apps or websites, or share Bluetooth observations with third parties. Visual angles on the signal map are layout positions and are not measured bearings.

Retention and deletion

Temporary Bluetooth signal records are held only in memory. Any preview timestamp left by an earlier version remains unused in local preferences until the app is removed. App Store purchase records, if any, are maintained by Apple. Because Signal Scout has no developer account or server, the developer has no server-side user record to delete.

Children

Signal Scout is a general-audience utility and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the effective date and this page will be updated before new data practices are introduced. For privacy questions, use the email contact or public support channel on the Support page. Do not include private device identifiers or other sensitive information in a public issue.
