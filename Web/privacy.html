PRIVACY POLICY

Private by design.

Privacy policy for version 1.4 (build 15) · Updated November 10, 2026

Summary

Signal Scout processes Bluetooth device names, advertised manufacturer identifiers, and signal readings on your device. It does not transmit these observations to the developer, sell them, or share them. The app has no developer-operated server, account system, analytics SDK, or advertising SDK. Optional Wi-Fi identification uses Apple's location authorization solely to request the connected network identity.

Bluetooth information

With your permission, Signal Scout receives Bluetooth Low Energy advertisements that iOS makes available to apps. It uses the advertised or iOS-reported device name, the company identifier in manufacturer-specific data, an app-scoped identifier, signal strength (RSSI), timestamps, and advertised service count to display and analyze nearby devices.

Device names and manufacturer identifiers are displayed and retained only in memory during the scan session. Names may contain labels set by the device owner. The remaining manufacturer-specific payload is not retained. These records are not written to a database or sent off the device. They disappear when the app process ends and can be removed from the current session with the Clear Inactive Devices control.

Free access and earlier versions

The free release has no preview timer, subscription purchase, or StoreKit entitlement check. All features are available without payment. It does not read or write a preview start time.

Earlier versions stored a preview start time only in local app preferences and used Apple's StoreKit for subscriptions. A timestamp left by an earlier installation is ignored by this version and is removed when the app is deleted. Apple maintains any existing App Store purchase records under Apple's privacy policy; Signal Scout does not receive or store payment-card details.

Wi-Fi and location permission

Connection status is available without location permission. If you choose Show Wi-Fi name, iOS requires precise location authorization before it may disclose the connected network's name (SSID), identifier (BSSID), and security information. Signal Scout does not request geographic coordinates, scan nearby access points, or save or upload network identities. You can decline this permission and continue using Bluetooth and connection status. Network identity is cleared when hidden or when you leave Wi-Fi mode.

Optional sensors

Magnetic-field, pressure, relative-altitude, and sound measurements start only when you choose Start. Motion or microphone permission may be required. Sound is a relative digital level (dBFS), not calibrated sound-pressure level. Microphone samples are processed on-device and are not recorded to files or uploaded. Sensor readings and bounded recent histories stay in memory; leaving Sensors or backgrounding the app stops measurements and clears those readings.

Tracking and sharing

Signal Scout does not track you across apps or websites or share Bluetooth, network, or sensor observations with third parties. Visual angles on the signal map are layout positions and are not measured bearings. iOS Settings lets you change Bluetooth, Location, Microphone, and Motion permissions.

Retention and deletion

Temporary Bluetooth signal records, optional Wi-Fi identities, and sensor histories are held only in memory. Any preview timestamp left by an earlier version remains unused in local preferences until the app is removed. App Store purchase records, if any, are maintained by Apple. Because Signal Scout has no developer account or server, the developer has no server-side user record to delete.

Children

Signal Scout is a general-audience utility and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the effective date and this page will be updated before new data practices are introduced. For privacy questions, use the email contact or public support channel on the Support page. Do not include private device identifiers or other sensitive information in a public issue.
