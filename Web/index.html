SIGNAL SCOUT

Find lost headphones and Bluetooth devices.

Signal Scout maps anonymous Bluetooth Low Energy advertisements by relative signal strength, then gives you warmer-and-colder guidance as you move to narrow your search.

Privacy PolicyHow the app handles Bluetooth and purchase data. SupportTroubleshooting, subscriptions, and contact options.

The item must be actively advertising over Bluetooth Low Energy. Signal strength is approximate; ordinary Bluetooth RSSI cannot provide a true compass bearing or exact physical distance.
