Signal Scout help

No signals appear

Confirm that Bluetooth is enabled and that Signal Scout is allowed under Settings → Privacy & Security → Bluetooth. Only Bluetooth Low Energy devices that are actively advertising can appear.

A signal is not identifiable

Signal Scout intentionally hides device names and manufacturer information. Move the device you control close to the phone and look for the anonymous signal whose dBm value becomes stronger.

The map direction looks wrong

The map does not claim directional measurement. Distance from the center reflects relative RSSI; the angle is a stable visual lane used to separate signals. Ordinary iPhone Bluetooth RSSI does not provide a compass bearing.

Restore or manage a subscription

Use Restore Purchases on the subscription screen if you already subscribed with the current Apple Account. Manage or cancel through Apple's subscription settings.

Contact support

Open a support request through Signal Scout Support Issues. Do not post private device identifiers, payment information, precise locations, or other sensitive information. Billing and refund requests must be handled through Apple.
